Cluttered Closet: Escape

You walk in and the door closes behind you. The first clue flashes on the screen. Find all ten clues and get out.

Keys: WASD or arrows to move,
E to grab, drop, and open things
Move cursor to move the screen (first person game)
Mouse extras: left-click also grabs/opens, hold right-click to walk forward. C opens your clue notebook.

Paused

Click anywhere to get back in the room.
